# Literature map: Loot or Love?

Searched 10 Nov 2026 with Consensus (Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex and PubMed) and web search. ★ marks a work cited in the paper. Everything here was actually found, and each entry has a link.

**Bottom line:** nothing published races a game drop rate against partner search. Each half of the model does have a literature, and the paper now cites the closest work on each side.

## 1. The method we borrow
- ★ [Backus (2010), *Why I don't have a girlfriend*](https://www.astro.sunysb.edu/fwalter/AST248/why_i_dont_have_a_girlfriend.pdf). This is where the filter chain comes from.
- ★ [Mortensen (1988), *Matching: Finding a partner for life or otherwise*](https://consensus.app/papers/details/fd7fc463a0495b7a82ae0dd6570d493b/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *American Journal of Sociology*. The economics of search with frictions. Ours is the simplest case.
- ★ [Chow et al. (1964), the secretary problem](https://consensus.app/papers/details/17974168e9c55fafa9d65fb1ce6e5098/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Israel J. Math*. Optimal stopping.
- ★ [Eriksson et al. (2004), two-sided secretary problem](https://consensus.app/papers/details/99d20840384b5a089453b79e3dcc7c63/?utm_source=claude_desktop), arXiv. Men and women search at the same time.
- [Dombrovsky & Perrin (1994), adaptive search in mate choice](https://consensus.app/papers/details/f19d0bf6d4d750df8015c3dbe5bdeabd/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *American Naturalist*. A biology version of the same problem.

## 2. The 5:49 rule (foraging)
- ★ [Charnov (1976), marginal value theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marginal_value_theorem). The 5:49 rule is this theorem.
- ★ [Saravanakumar et al. (2024), patch-leaving in humans and gerbils](https://consensus.app/papers/details/e03f2bd7afeb58b8ac010dcf5a2f2f1d/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Communications Biology*. Some humans overharvest, which is the Fortnite player who stays in to win.
- [Hong et al. (2025), patches half-empty](https://consensus.app/papers/details/271ea677648352a8a3e87240ddb859e0/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Journal of Vision*. People leave good patches early when some patches are empty.
- [Webb et al. (2024), MVT with Bayesian updating](https://consensus.app/papers/details/06b08bdac22b5d3fb68f1812eee63272/?utm_source=claude_desktop), bioRxiv. Mice follow the theorem even when the environment is uncertain.

## 3. Rare drops and how players perceive them
- ★ [Larche et al. (2019), rare loot box rewards trigger larger arousal](https://consensus.app/papers/details/503f42a3f83d5a67aa398353a2e8aa70/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Journal of Gambling Studies*
- ★ [Wen et al. (2024), folk models of loot boxes](https://consensus.app/papers/details/f2b0845b0048559fabcb4cf577ad1337/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *PACM HCI*. Players misunderstand drop odds even when they're published.
- ★ [Cordes et al. (2024), what drives demand for loot boxes](https://consensus.app/papers/details/df59748161485a31b9bed50fda9aca5f/?utm_source=claude_desktop), SSRN. Opaque odds and showing only the winners double what people will pay. This is the "only lucky people post" effect.
- ★ [Xiao et al. (2023), Opening Pandora's loot box](https://consensus.app/papers/details/7e6de9e2f03f541cb4331c352edd0d7f/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Journal of Gambling Studies*. Probability disclosures in China.
- [Xiao et al. (2021), suboptimal compliance with disclosure rules](https://consensus.app/papers/details/5efb507aa9375be9a52e11b10fe00012/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Behavioural Public Policy*
- [Zendle & Cairns (2018), loot boxes and problem gambling](https://consensus.app/papers/details/2b2748764fa158d78efb5c76f74419c5/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *PLoS ONE*. Not cited, because the token comes from free chests and isn't paid.

## 4. Battle royale, skill and matchmaking
- ★ [Rosenbusch et al. (2020), battle royale results are mostly luck](https://consensus.app/papers/details/2a6a7c0b979155b590b56c8af3385c8b/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Simulation & Gaming*
- ★ Sapienza et al. (2018), performance declines over a session, *Royal Society Open Science* (already cited)
- [Dehpanah et al. (2021), team skill aggregation in battle royale](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c611f20e1a045e719aaf43f8180342a1/?utm_source=claude_desktop), IEEE CoG
- [Kang et al. (2024), matchmaking and churn](https://consensus.app/papers/details/897059379e2852538cc1b25de4c0f852/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Heliyon*. Tougher lobbies make players quit.

## 5. Player counts
- ★ [Vihanga et al. (2019), weekly player-population patterns](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c9730d8e22ab5774a553153a633b1472/?utm_source=claude_desktop), IEEE CoG
- ★ [Henderson & Bhatti (2001), modelling user behaviour in networked games](https://consensus.app/papers/details/44813f05efbe5c18b07ed4be7c0ab608/?utm_source=claude_desktop), ACM Multimedia

## 6. Online dating
- ★ [Bruch & Newman (2018), aspirational pursuit of mates](https://consensus.app/papers/details/7b3df81815e951a2b52c1dec137bb60a/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Science Advances*. People message about 25% "above" themselves, and reply rates fall the further up they aim.
- [Kreager et al. (2014), "Where have all the good men gone?"](https://consensus.app/papers/details/9d9bf05d5ddb54cda570f739b38f1463/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Journal of Marriage and Family*
- [Lindsay et al., "Out of my league"](https://consensus.app/papers/details/d79d94e3e8c35605a8ccb1a98aebd755/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *PSPB*. A real-world test of the matching hypothesis.
- ★ Tyson et al. (2016), Tinder; Rudder (2009), OkCupid; Rosenfeld et al. (2019), how couples meet (already cited)

## 7. Height
- ★ NCHS (2025), measured heights from 2021 to 2023: men aged 20 to 29 average 176.3 cm ([PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/174595/cdc_174595_DS1.pdf))
- ★ [Hitsch, Hortaçsu & Ariely (2006)](https://docs.asian-nation.org/online-dating-study.pdf). Men who are 6'3"–6'4" get 65% more first messages than men who are 5'7"–5'8".
- ★ [Hensley (1994), height and attraction](https://consensus.app/papers/details/b68b51c904395445960717e72a5c045a/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Adolescence*. The advantage levels off above 6 ft.
- ★ [Witmer et al. (2025), conjoint study of dating attributes](https://consensus.app/papers/details/b84b50c5cb6b5574a1429adf09687399/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *CHB Reports*. Height counts for 7 to 20 times less than looks.
- [Yancey & Emerson (2016), does height matter?](https://consensus.app/papers/details/448662f6a4f05292a09b069bc636e016/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Journal of Family Issues*
- [Ong (2015), height and income field experiment](https://consensus.app/papers/details/b558a2883330544d91a6f8b7cde40b50/?utm_source=claude_desktop)
- [Toma et al., deception in online dating profiles](https://consensus.app/papers/details/72b41ed7af5955dc97b108e807f33b67/?utm_source=claude_desktop). Men lie most about height. This makes a good footnote: the 65% is based on height as men report it.

## 8. Does gaming displace dating?
- ★ [Kowert et al. (2014), "Social gaming, lonely life?"](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c38e5d5217e558de8e2c6011a91e502a/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Computers in Human Behavior*. Supports displacement.
- ★ [Domahidi et al. (2018), longitudinal study of online players](https://consensus.app/papers/details/8082a1a7097550d7912f15f44d23c35d/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Media Psychology*. Finds no displacement.
- [Ding et al. (2023), video games and youth romantic relationships](https://consensus.app/papers/details/e6aeeb43701357b8b03dd227c8c81e8f/?utm_source=claude_desktop)

## 9. Breakups (one of the two most important parameters)
- ★ [Le et al. (2010), meta-analysis of nonmarital breakups](https://consensus.app/papers/details/af53abf5bda85e90bc95ec31f75f5311/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Personal Relationships*. It covers 137 studies, but they're about what predicts a breakup, not how often breakups happen.
- [Joyner et al. (2017), stability of young adult relationships](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c2ae6457abc75ae4ae00ca1b299ffa94/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Demography*. Add Health hazard models, which are the best source for a real breakup rate in a later version.
- [Lantagne & Furman (2017), stay or leave](https://consensus.app/papers/details/8ca14f353beb5251a81e660eebf89525/?utm_source=claude_desktop), *Emerging Adulthood*
