Built on the model from Loot or Love? Will a Fortnite Player Find the Pirate Shop Token Before a Girlfriend? (D. Thompson, 2026). Token odds are Epic's 1 in 137,500 per chest. The dating side is calibrated to Pew and Stanford survey data, but things like the DM reply rate and the effort multipliers are guesses. The paper tests how much they matter. The paper's headline 2.5% is the average over all guys; a guy at the 50th percentile for everything comes out a bit lower, because a few very lucky guys drag the average up.
